CareerTrust AI

Hackathon · Team PHOENIX · Team lead

Problem

Students lose money and personal data to fake job and internship offers, and there is no quick way to check one before replying.

What I built

Paste an offer or upload a screenshot. A rule-based engine with 13 scam rules returns a risk score with plain-language reasons. It also checks salary fairness, skill match and company details, flags sensitive data such as Aadhaar and PAN numbers, and compares several offers side by side.

Product details

Multi-page React app, Tamil and English UI, voice input, dark mode, report history and PDF export.

Sovereign Trust Shield

Hackathon · HackFusion · built in 24 hours

Problem

In "Digital Arrest" scams, the caller keeps the victim on the line and pushes them to transfer money before they can think.

What I built

The call screen turns speech into text on the device and matches it against known threat phrases. When a scam pattern appears, the banking screen locks and an alarm sounds before any transfer goes through. A Socket.io server keeps the two screens in sync.

Shipped

Deployed publicly on Vercel, so anyone can open the demo.

CallGuard AI

Hackathon · VELOHACK26 · FinTech and AI/ML

Problem

Scammers stay on the phone and ask victims to read out the OTP the bank just sent. Each signal alone looks harmless.

What I designed

A system that combines call status, an incoming OTP, caller verification and speech keywords into one AI risk score, and warns the user while the call is still live. The prototype is a web dashboard simulation, with an Android app as the real-world target.

Next ideas

Fake bank identity detection, scam detection in Tamil, English and Hindi, one-tap reporting and a post-call safety check.

Real-Time Safe Route Mapping

College project · PDL-III · In progress

Problem

Navigation apps pick the fastest route and say little about how risky a road is.

What I am building

An ESP32 with a GPS module and a motion sensor collects live road data. A Flask backend compares Logistic Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest models, then OSMnx finds a safer route that is drawn on a map.

Ground rule

Real data only. No invented accident records and no made-up results. Hardware budget is under ₹2,000.

  • NEO-6M GPS
